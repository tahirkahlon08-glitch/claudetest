Our Story

More than a dessert. It's an experience.

A good dessert is rarely eaten alone. It is passed across the table, compared, shared and remembered.

At Aseer Time & Papa Kanafa, the menu is built around that idea: fresh drinks blended to order, warm kanafa, crepes and waffles, and chocolate creations that make an ordinary evening feel like an occasion.

Come with friends, bring the family, and discover a flavour you did not know you were craving.
